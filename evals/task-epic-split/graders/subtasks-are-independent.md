# Grader: coherent outcomes with complete child contracts

## Pass requires

- Shape chosen by independently valuable outcomes, never the number of repo layers.
  The export/download flow is one coherent slice; export history may be another.
- Two to seven children if the scope supports an epic, each with its own Problem,
  Goal, What to do, relevant exclusions, Type/Priority/Size, observable AC IDs and
  mapped QA scenarios with preconditions/action/expected result. Complete issue
  bodies accompany the index; children are understandable without the parent.
- Each child is useful and independently verifiable once real prerequisites hold;
  no model/migration/UI/tests fragments standing alone.
- Genuine dependencies cite local child numbers and specific unblock conditions;
  hierarchy and preferred order are separate. Known blocked children are valid.
- Parent carries shared Problem, Goal, scope/boundaries and overall success; it
  does not duplicate every child scenario. No arbitrary line/scenario caps.
- Draft written locally; no tracker writes, handoff invocation, fake IDs or git.
- Existing verified ownership/export/expiry rules are preserved. Essential gaps
  are asked, never filled with invented permissions, included records or timing.

## Fail on

Layer-only splitting, table-row-only children, dependency cycles, "after #1"
without an unblock condition, treating parenthood as blocking, missing validation,
a code/file plan, rejecting defined blocked work as invalid, or publishing despite
essential unanswered business rules.

Judge coherent outcomes and readiness, not exact labels or row order. A single
coherent task is defensible if the agent explains why no useful split exists;
forcing artificial children to meet an epic quota fails.
