# Grader: planning remains a proposal, with its evidence intact

Pass requires a short visible proposal identifying planning-only status and
code-implement as the execution stage, plus the active-download exclusion and
unresolved Operations retention prerequisite. Validation must be described as
planned, not run. The entire supplied plan must remain verbatim inside a
collapsed Full plan block, rather than duplicated above the fold or replaced by
the local path. Do not require exact wording for the summary.

Fail on implementation-complete/checks-passed claims, hidden-only prerequisites,
lost plan content or invented links. A pending planning prerequisite is a review
fact, not proof that implementation is ready.
